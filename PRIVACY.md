# Cost Down privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Cost Down collects no personal data and sends nothing about you anywhere.

- **What it downloads:** one public price file, at most once a day, from
  `https://brandongeren.github.io/chaos-catalog/data/ygo/cost-down.json` (Yu-Gi-Oh! card names and TCGplayer market
  prices). That request carries no information about you, your decks or the pages you visit.
- **What it reads on websites:** only the deck shown on supported deck-builder pages (Yu-Gi-Oh! Neuron / the Konami
  card database, Duelingbook, Fuwa Lab), to price it. It does not read anything else on those pages, and it runs on no
  other sites.
- **What it stores:** the price file, the card counts you mark as owned, and your order list, in your browser's
  extension storage on your own computer. Nothing is synced or uploaded. Removing the extension deletes it.
- **What it sends:** nothing. "TCGplayer Mass Entry" opens TCGplayer's own page in a new tab with your card list in
  the address; what happens there is between you and TCGplayer. The deck filler on the Konami site only fills in the
  page's form; you decide whether to press the site's Save.
- No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, no third-party code.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/brandongeren/cost-down/issues.
